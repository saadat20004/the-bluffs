RV & Tent Site

A small campground with a whole farm around it

Eight sites sit along Cedar Creek as it winds through the middle of an 863 acre farm. Six of them are right on the water, and each one is level, grassy and easy to pull into.

You're about 1.5 hours from Washington DC and 4 miles from the West Virginia line. Wildlife is everywhere, the nights are quiet, and you can park right at your site so unloading takes minutes.

On the Farm & Nearby

Plenty to do, or nothing at all

Most guests say it's hard to leave the property. When you do, the Shenandoah Valley is right outside the gate.

On site

4 Mile Trail

Walk or run from one end of the farm to the other and loop back without retracing a step.

On site

Trout Stream

Cedar Creek is stocked with trout and has deep pools for a cool swim. Pond fishing is free.

On site

The Pond

A small sandy beach with kayaks and a paddle boat. Kids love it on hot summer days.

2 miles

Eagle Rock

A short drive to one of the best hikes in the area, with a wide view back to the east.

Under 1 hr

Shenandoah NP

Skyline Drive, waterfalls and big ridgeline hikes, all within an easy day trip.

Nearby

Valley Tastings

Farmers markets, wineries, breweries and even a distillery just down the valley.
